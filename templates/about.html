Virtual Laboratory for Synchronous Machines

The Electrical Machine Virtual Laboratory is an interactive educational platform designed for engineering students. It aims to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application by providing a realistic simulation environment for determining the voltage regulation of synchronous machines.

Traditional laboratory sessions often involve tedious graph plotting and complex calculations. This virtual lab automates these processes while maintaining the educational value by animating the step-by-step construction of graphs exactly as taught in textbooks.

Methods Covered

  • EMF Method (Synchronous Impedance Method): A pessimistic method using OCC and SCC to find synchronous impedance.
  • MMF Method (Ampere-Turn Method): An optimistic method that treats all voltage drops as MMFs.
  • ZPF Method (Potier Triangle Method): The most accurate method that separates armature leakage reactance and armature reaction effects.

Technologies Used

  • Frontend: HTML5, Vanilla CSS (Custom Variables, Flexbox, Grid), JavaScript (ES6+).
  • Graphing Engine: Plotly.js for interactive, animated, and high-performance charting.
  • Backend: Python Flask for serving templates and providing a scalable architecture.
  • UI/UX: Dark theme, fully responsive dashboard, FontAwesome icons, Google Fonts.
