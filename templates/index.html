Available Experiments

LAB 1

EMF Method

Synchronous Impedance Method. Calculates voltage regulation by combining the open-circuit excitation curves and short-circuit linear curves to identify synchronous impedance ($Z_s$) at rated conditions.

Launch Lab
LAB 2

MMF Method

Ampere-Turn Method. Combines no-load excitation field requirements ($I_{f0}$) and equivalent short-circuit armature reaction field requirements ($I_{f\_sc}$) vectorially to obtain high physical accuracy.

Launch Lab
LAB 3

ZPF Method

Potier Triangle Method. Separates the leakage reactance voltage drop ($I X_l$) and equivalent armature reaction MMF ($F_{ar}$) utilizing the Potier triangle, representing the gold standard of regulator estimation.
