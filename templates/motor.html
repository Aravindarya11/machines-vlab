PRIMARY FUNCTION:

TECHNICAL ENCYCLOPEDIA ANALYSIS:

The stator core is built from thin, insulated laminations of silicon steel to minimize eddy current losses. The inner slots house three-phase AC windings arranged 120° electrical degrees apart. When energized, they produce a continuous magnetic field rotating at synchronous speed (Ns).
